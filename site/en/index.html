How it works

From the first call to the keys

  1. 01

    First call

    A video call to understand your budget, timeline and the life you want here. No commitment.

  2. 02

    Shortlist and live visits

    A shortlist that fits, with honest notes on each area. I visit with you on live video, so you see the home before you travel.

  3. 03

    Tax number and bank

    You'll need a Portuguese tax number (NIF) and usually a bank account. I connect you with trusted lawyers and translators who handle it remotely.

  4. 04

    Offer and contract

    I negotiate for you. Then comes the promissory contract (CPCV), signed with a deposit, which secures the home.

  5. 05

    Deed and keys

    The final deed is signed at a notary, in person or through your lawyer with power of attorney. I hand you the keys and help with utilities after you move in.

FAQ

Questions people ask me

Can I buy a home in Portugal without being there?

Yes. With a power of attorney, a lawyer can sign the contracts and the deed for you. Many of my clients only travel once the home is theirs.

Do I need a Portuguese tax number (NIF)?

Yes, to buy or rent. As a non-resident you can get one through a lawyer or tax representative, usually within a few days.

Can foreigners get a mortgage in Portugal?

Yes. Portuguese banks lend to non-residents, usually a lower share of the price than to residents. I can put you in touch with credit intermediaries who compare banks for you.

How long does buying take?

It depends on financing and paperwork. Without a mortgage it is often a matter of weeks from offer to deed. With a mortgage, plan for a few months.

Do you also help with rentals?

Yes. I help tenants find a home and owners rent theirs out, with the contract and the hand-over of keys.

What does it cost me as a buyer?

My fee is usually paid by the seller. I'll always tell you upfront if a service has a cost.
